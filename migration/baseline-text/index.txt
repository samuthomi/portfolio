About
I find out why users drop off, then design the fix. I've done this for eight years across fintech, clean energy, logistics, marketplaces and internal tools, mostly where a broken flow costs money and users rarely give you a second chance.
At AMI, I made each stage of the CapPlus diagnostic funnel visible. Conversion to a completed diagnostic rose from about 36% to around 50% by September 2026, a gain shared with marketing and sourcing. AMI's Solutions team now uses the same funnel structure on other projects.
I lead while I design. At Sendy I grew from visual designer to Product Design Lead, built the design system for all of Sendy's apps from scratch, and managed three designers while still owning payments design. At KOKO Networks I led another team of three while leading the stock take app redesign.
I work best with teams whose users sign up but don't stay, or who want evidence before committing to a big build.
